How it works

A short guide to what happens, and what to expect.

Three checks per question

  1. Write: one AI call drafts an original question and worked solution.
  2. Solve blind: another call solves it without seeing the answer. If the answers differ, the question is discarded.
  3. Audit: a strict physics review checks units, options and ambiguity. Numerical answers are also recomputed in code.

What to expect on the free plan

  • Roughly a minute per verified question.
  • Google's free limits are small. If the daily limit is reached, you keep what was verified so far.
  • Picking several chapters makes one paper, but each chapter is generated one after another, so it takes longer than a single-chapter paper of the same size.
  • The server sleeps when idle and forgets everything. That is why papers are saved in your browser.

Before you use a paper

  • Skim every question and the answer key. AI checking is strong but not perfect.
  • Use the bin icon to remove any question you do not like. It is removed from the PDFs too.
  • Set the title, duration and marking in Paper settings.
